# One paper, one learning flow

Start with a centered upload screen: PDF upload and a visible URL/DOI field, compact sample/resume actions, document history and practice points. Root and retired Research Lab links open this home.

The primary flow is **add paper → inspect extracted text → confirm processing → answer challenges → inspect evidence and feedback → review findings**. Keep one central task and a clear next action at each step.

Without AI credentials, real PDF checking offers an explicit, separately labeled sample lesson. Sample findings never claim to come from the upload. Link preview supports only the curated DOI; arbitrary URL import remains pending.

Ten practice points are awarded once per solved challenge in each adventure. They record participation, not validated understanding or mastery. Refreshes and explanation edits do not award extra points.

## Reasoning demonstration

The optional `/#/research/reasoning` preview shows a choice, explanation, evidence, revision and comparison without an API. Prepared examples receive labeled authored feedback; custom writing stays unassessed. It demonstrates the intended mechanism without presenting a fabricated model review. The duplicate standalone paper-case application has been retired.

For a demo, upload a PDF, inspect its text, enter the explicit sample lesson, answer a challenge, review feedback, then show saved points and resume. Use the reasoning preview to explain how a matching choice can still contain a misconception.

## Integration and evaluation

Live review must use the exact paper, permitted evidence and current explanation revision, reject unsupported or stale feedback, and preserve recoverable failures. See [REASONING_PREVIEW.md](REASONING_PREVIEW.md) and [RESEARCH_INTEGRATION.md](RESEARCH_INTEGRATION.md).

Expert content review and learner studies are still needed. Test whether learners can explain the question, approach, findings and limitations and transfer the distinction to a new study. Software checks, confidence and points do not establish a learning benefit.
