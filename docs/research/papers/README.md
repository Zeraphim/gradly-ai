# Stored source papers

Source PDFs kept alongside [../PAPER_SELECTION.md](../PAPER_SELECTION.md). This folder
holds **only** files whose licence clearly permits redistribution. Free-to-read papers
whose terms do not grant redistribution (arXiv preprints, PMLR proceedings) stay as links
in the shortlist and are never copied here.

Each entry records where the file came from, its version, and its licence, and the
stored file's own text is checked against the citation it claims. Before adding a file,
confirm the PDF you actually have matches the work named here: read its first page and
embedded metadata, not just the download link. `tests/research/stored-papers.test.ts`
fails when a stored file's text does not carry the DOI or title its entry records, so a
wrong version or a different publication cannot be described as the cited work.

## Not stored, and why

- **Bobek & Tversky 2014** — the CogSci proceedings version of study 3, "Creating Visual
  Explanations Improves Learning" (eScholarship qt3gh2z11f), is a different publication
  from the 2016 journal article. The 2014 proceedings carry the Cognitive Science
  Society's "all rights reserved" notice with no redistribution licence, so the file is
  never stored; only its 2016 CC BY 4.0 journal version belongs under this folder.
- **Recht et al. 2019** (PMLR), **Lučić et al. 2018**, **Musgrave et al. 2020**, and
  **Ferrari Dacrema et al. 2019** (arXiv) are free to read, but PMLR author copyright and
  arXiv's default non-exclusive licence do not grant third-party redistribution. Linked
  only.
- **Deslauriers et al. 2019** (_PNAS_, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) is licensed for redistribution but
  every retrieval route (PNAS, PMC, Europe PMC) blocked automated download.
- **Miller & Sanjurjo 2018** (arXiv:1902.01265) and **Watts et al. 2018** (PMC author
  manuscript) have no redistribution licence verified.
- The paywalled versions of record (Roediger & Karpicke; Rohrer & Taylor; Chi et al.;
  Karpicke & Blunt) are never copied.
