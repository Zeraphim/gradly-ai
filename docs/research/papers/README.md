# Stored source papers

Source PDFs kept alongside [../PAPER_SELECTION.md](../PAPER_SELECTION.md). This folder
holds **only** files whose licence clearly permits redistribution. Free-to-read papers
whose terms do not grant redistribution (arXiv preprints, PMLR proceedings) stay as links
in the shortlist and are never copied here.

Each entry records where the file came from, its version, and its licence.

## Not stored, and why

- **Recht et al. 2019** (PMLR), **Lučić et al. 2018**, **Musgrave et al. 2020**, and
  **Ferrari Dacrema et al. 2019** (arXiv) are free to read, but PMLR author copyright and
  arXiv's default non-exclusive licence do not grant third-party redistribution. Linked
  only.
- **Deslauriers et al. 2019** (*PNAS*, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) is licensed for redistribution but
  every retrieval route (PNAS, PMC, Europe PMC) blocked automated download.
- **Miller & Sanjurjo 2018** (arXiv:1902.01265) and **Watts et al. 2018** (PMC author
  manuscript) have no redistribution licence verified.
- The paywalled versions of record (Roediger & Karpicke; Rohrer & Taylor; Chi et al.;
  Karpicke & Blunt) are never copied.
